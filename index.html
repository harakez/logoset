Questions

Why do I need an SVG file?

SVG is the vector format logos are made in. It scales to any size without blur, and its colors can be changed. A PNG or JPG can't be recolored or enlarged cleanly.

I don't have an SVG. Where do I get one?

Ask the designer who made your logo. It's usually among the files you received: look for .svg, .ai or .eps. Illustrator, Figma and Inkscape can all export SVG.

Why can't I change the color of separate parts?

Some logos are saved as one combined shape, so there is nothing to separate and the logo can only be recolored as a whole. Try the "By part" mode first. If that doesn't help, ask for an SVG with the parts kept as separate shapes.

What size should a profile picture be?

1080 × 1080 pixels works for Instagram, Telegram, WhatsApp and most other apps. They crop avatars to a circle, so the logo has to stay inside it. Logoset takes care of that.

What is the YouTube banner size?

2560 × 1440 pixels. Only the middle area of 1546 × 423 is visible on every device, so the logo should sit inside it.

Is it free? Do you keep my logo?

It's free and open source. Everything happens in your browser and the logo isn't uploaded anywhere.
